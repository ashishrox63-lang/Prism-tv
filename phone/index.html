Remote

Connecting to your TV

Swipe to move. Tap to select.
Press and hold for options.

Arrows and OK work on Prism. In other apps, Back, Home, play and volume work. Arrows also work in other apps' text boxes when Prism Keyboard is on.

"Search on TV" types into Prism's search. "Send to the open app" types into Netflix, YouTube or any app's text box, once Prism Keyboard is turned on (Settings tab).

Photos show in the screensaver. They are resized on your phone and stored only on the TV.
